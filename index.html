About VisitLK


VisitLK is Sri Lanka's local stay marketplace — built for Sri Lankan hosts and travellers who want to go beyond five-star hotels. We connect guests with authentic homestays, heritage bungalows, eco-camps, and temple retreats across every district of the island.


Host Verification

Every host on VisitLK completes identity verification including NIC, address, and phone confirmation — so you always know who you're staying with.


Reservation Screening

Our system reviews every booking request and flags high-risk reservations before they're confirmed, protecting hosts and guests alike.


LKR-First Pricing

All stays are priced in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). USD equivalent is shown for international guests. We support local payment methods including FriMi, Genie, and bank transfer.
